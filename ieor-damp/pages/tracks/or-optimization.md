## Who it suits

- You liked the proof-heavy parts of your optimization and probability courses, not just the applications.
- You are considering an MS or PhD (in India or abroad) in OR, industrial engineering, operations management or applied probability.
- You can commit to a demanding elective load and a long research project.

## Semester by semester

Codes come from IEOR electives that IITB students have taken (see [Courses]({{ '/courses/' | relative_url }})). Check which semester each one actually runs in before you register.

| Semester | Courses | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 3 (Autumn) | IEOR core; **IE 501** Optimization Models if the slot allows | Modelling foundation |
| 4 (Spring) | IEOR core; **IE 630** Simulation Modeling and Analysis | Stochastic modelling |
| 5 (Autumn) | **IE 609** Mathematical Optimization Techniques, **IE 621** Probability and Stochastic Processes I | The theory most OR grad programmes assume |
| 6 (Spring) | **IE 622** Probability and Stochastic Processes II, **IE 616** Decision Analysis and Game Theory | Depth; pick one if load is high |
| 7 (Autumn) | **IE 708** Markov Decision Processes; BTP starts | Research-level course |
| 8 (Spring) | BTP continues; one more advanced elective | Finish with a strong project |

## Minor or honours

- **Honours in IEOR** fits this track best, because the extra courses are the advanced ones above. Confirm the honours rules for your batch first (see [FAQs]({{ '/faq/#minors' | relative_url }})).
- A **Mathematics** minor (real analysis, linear algebra, measure-theoretic probability) also suits you if you are aiming for theory-heavy PhD programmes.

## Skills to build alongside

- LP/IP modelling in a solver (Gurobi, CPLEX or an open-source one such as HiGHS) through Python.
- Reading papers: pick one area, and read the papers your SLP guide works from.
- Writing a short research report in LaTeX.

## Research experience

- Do an **SLP in 2nd or 3rd year** with an IEOR prof. See the [Radar entry on finding a guide]({{ '/radar/finding-slp-btp-guide/' | relative_url }}).
- Apply for a research internship in the summer after 3rd year (DAAD WISE, Mitacs, Summer@EPFL; see the [Radar]({{ '/radar/?kind=research' | relative_url }})).
- Three recommendation letters is the usual ask for grad school. Plan for them from 3rd year.

## Honest effort note

This is the hardest track. If your CPI is suffering, drop one elective per semester rather than doing everything badly. A strong SLP counts for more than an extra course.
